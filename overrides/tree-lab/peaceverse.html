Four distinct ecosystem indicators

This prototype does not run i-Tree. Tree inventory, verified species, trunk diameter and suitable local weather/pollution data are required. Istanbul and species/model applicability have not been verified.

Carbon stored

Standing stock in tree tissues, in kg C or explicitly converted kg CO₂. Do not add it to annual sequestration.

Annual carbon sequestration

A modeled yearly rate, in kg C/year or kg CO₂/year, separate from the stock.

Air pollutant removal

Mass per pollutant per year. Not the same as CO₂, measured air quality or a health outcome.

Avoided surface runoff

A modeled volume in m³/year. Not the same as rainfall intercepted and not drinking-water savings.

No results yet. Future estimates must retain source, model version, location, period, units and uncertainty. Measured inputs and modeled outputs stay separate.

SPRING 2027 · PROPOSED PLAN

Our spring plan

MARCH / DISCOVER

Know the garden

Survey existing trees, verify species with expert support and collect baseline observations. Exact dates are not agreed yet.

APRIL / DESIGN

Nameplates and identities

Choose names together, design wooden labels and connect QR pages. Use independent stakes, not nails in trunks.

MAY / REFLECT

Measure, reflect, share

Share care stories and reflections on cooperation, inclusion and responsibility. Environmental records remain optional and evidence-based.

BEFORE SHARING

Keep participation safe

No student names, faces or private information. Real records, school authorization and the sharing audience still need preparation.
